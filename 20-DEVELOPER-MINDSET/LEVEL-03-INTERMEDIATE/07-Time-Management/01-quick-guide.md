# Time Management

## 1. What is it?
Manage your coding study sessions using time-blocking, scheduling, and minimizing distractions.

## 2. Why do we use it?
To block dedicated distraction-free time slots and use techniques like the Pomodoro method to maintain long-term focus.

## 3. How does it work?
- **Analogy**: Training for a marathon. You schedule dedicated, focused blocks, tracking metrics to keep consistency.
- **Mechanism**: The feature parses inputs or operations synchronously or asynchronously, executing compiled instructions and returning output responses or state modifications.

## 4. Where is it used?
Structuring daily study calendars.

## 5. How do we build with it?
```json
Using Pomodoro timers: 25 minutes of focused coding, followed by a 5-minute break.
```

- **Expected Output**: Reduces burnout risk and increases daily coding progress.
- **Best Practice / Rule**: Two hours of focused coding with your phone muted beats 6 hours of distracted learning.
