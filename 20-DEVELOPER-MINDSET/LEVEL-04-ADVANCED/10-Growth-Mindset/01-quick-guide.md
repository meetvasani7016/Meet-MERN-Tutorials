# Growth Mindset in Tech

## 1. What is it?
A Growth Mindset is the belief that programming ability is built through persistence and effort, not talent.

## 2. Why do we use it?
To treat compiler crashes and interview rejections as learning milestones and opportunities to expand your skills.

## 3. How does it work?
- **Analogy**: Weightlifting. The muscle grows when you push against heavy resistance, not when you lift empty bars.
- **Mechanism**: The feature parses inputs or operations synchronously or asynchronously, executing compiled instructions and returning output responses or state modifications.

## 4. Where is it used?
Sustaining motivation across a lifelong engineering career.

## 5. How do we build with it?
```json
Treating a compiler error crash as a challenge to learn from, rather than a failure.
```

- **Expected Output**: Prevents impostor syndrome and keeps you motivated during tough bugs.
- **Best Practice / Rule**: Every expert developer was once a beginner who refused to quit when their code crashed.
