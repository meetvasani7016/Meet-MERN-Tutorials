To practice onboarding onto legacy code bases, asking structured debug questions, and converting student projects into corporate team contributions.

- **Mechanism**: The feature parses inputs or operations synchronously or asynchronously, executing compiled instructions and returning output responses or state modifications.

## 4. Where is it used?
Applying to internships and junior developer onboarding.

## 5. How do we build with it?
```json
Learning company codebases, asking structured debugging questions, and taking notes on workflows.
```

- **Expected Output**: Maximizes the chance of converting internships into full-time job offers.
