# Freelancing Basics

## 1. What is it?
How to find clients, scope deliverables, manage timelines, and build projects as a freelance web developer.

## 2. Why do we use it?
To learn how to scope deliverables, write service contracts, and deliver custom landing sites to local clients to earn money.

## 3. How does it work?
- **Analogy**: Running your own digital agency. You handle project scoping, client contracts, and technical builds.
- **Mechanism**: The feature parses inputs or operations synchronously or asynchronously, executing compiled instructions and returning output responses or state modifications.

## 4. Where is it used?
Freelancing channels, small business projects, and consulting.

```json
Building custom landing pages or catalog sites for local businesses.
```
