To optimize online profile keyword discoverability (SEO) so tech recruiters can find your profile for relevant roles.

- **Mechanism**: The feature parses inputs or operations synchronously or asynchronously, executing compiled instructions and returning output responses or state modifications.

## 4. Where is it used?
Online professional networking and outbound recruiting.
