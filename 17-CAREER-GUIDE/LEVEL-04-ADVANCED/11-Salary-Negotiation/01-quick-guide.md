To ensure fair compensation packages based on market rates, negotiate total packages (base, bonus, equity), and handle early salary questions professionally.

- **Analogy**: Professional negotiation. Understanding market value ranges, avoiding early salary disclosures, and proposing compromises.
- **Mechanism**: The feature parses inputs or operations synchronously or asynchronously, executing compiled instructions and returning output responses or state modifications.

## 4. Where is it used?
Reviewing job offers and final compensation negotiations.
