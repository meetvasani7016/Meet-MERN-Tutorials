# Summary: JavaScript Operators

Here is a quick cheat sheet of what we covered in this lesson:

* Arithmetic: +, -, *, /, % (remainder), ** (exponent).
* Assignment: =, +=, -=, *=, /=.
* Comparison: === (strict equals), !== (strict not equals), >, <, >=, <=.
* Logical: && (AND), || (OR), ! (NOT).
* Ternary: condition ? valueIfTrue : valueIfFalse.
