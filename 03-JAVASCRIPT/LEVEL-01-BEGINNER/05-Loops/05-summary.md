# Summary: JavaScript Loops

Here is a quick cheat sheet of what we covered in this lesson:

* for: best when you know the iteration count beforehand.
* while: best when looping until an state changes, regardless of counts.
* do...while: guarantees the block runs at least once before testing condition.
* break: immediately exits the entire loop block.
* continue: skips the remaining lines of the current iteration and jumps to the next loop step.
