# HR Interview Questions

## 1. What is it?
Common behavioral and HR questions for junior developers (using simple, structured answers).

## 2. Why do we use it?
To evaluate soft skills, team compatibility, conflict-resolution capacities, and growth mindset using structured STAR responses.

## 3. How does it work?
- **Analogy**: Preparing responses using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioral checkouts.
- **Mechanism**: The feature parses inputs or operations synchronously or asynchronously, executing compiled instructions and returning output responses or state modifications.

## 4. Where is it used?
Culture fit and behavioral interview rounds.

## 5. How do we build with it?
```json
Tell me about a time you struggled with code. -> Explain the problem, how you researched/debugged, and what you learned.
```

- **Expected Output**: Prepares you to communicate confidently with HR managers.
- **Best Practice / Rule**: Focus on learning capacity, teamwork, and problem-solving mindset, never badmouth former teammates.
