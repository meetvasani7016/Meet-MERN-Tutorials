# Summary: MongoDB CheatSheet

Here is a quick cheat sheet of what we covered in this lesson:

* CRUD: insertOne(), find(), updateOne(), deleteOne().
* Operators: $gt, $lt, $in, $or, $and, $set, $inc.
* Aggregation: $match, $group, $sort, $sum, $avg.
* Mongoose: mongoose.Schema(), mongoose.model().
