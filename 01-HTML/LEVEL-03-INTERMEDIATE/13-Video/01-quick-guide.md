# HTML Video

## In One Line
Video (<video>) embeds movies and visual media clips on a webpage.

## Think Like This
Video is a television set.

- The <video> tag is the visual screen.
- The 'controls' attribute represents the remote control buttons.

## Example
```html
<video src="movie.mp4" controls width="400"></video>
```

## Result
Renders a video screen player box.

## Remember
Modern browsers block video autoplay unless the video is marked 'muted' first.
