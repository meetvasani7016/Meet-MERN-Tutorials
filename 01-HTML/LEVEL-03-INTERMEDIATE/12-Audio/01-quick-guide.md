# HTML Audio

## In One Line
Audio (<audio>) is used to play sound files on a webpage.

## Think Like This
Audio is a cassette player mounted on a wall.

- The <audio> tag is the player casing.
- The 'controls' attribute adds the play/pause volume buttons.
- The <source> is the cassette tape loaded inside.

## Example
```html
<audio src="sound.mp3" controls></audio>
```

## Result
Renders a sound player bar with play, volume, and seek controls.

## Remember
You must include the 'controls' attribute, or the player will be invisible!
