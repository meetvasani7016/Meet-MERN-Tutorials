Grandma's Secret Chocolate Chip Cookies

These cookies are crispy on the edges and chewy in the center.

Ingredients Needed

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup white granulated sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cream butter and sugar until smooth.
  3. Beat in the eggs, then mix in flour and chocolate chips.
  4. Drop large spoonfuls onto cookie sheets.
  5. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes.
