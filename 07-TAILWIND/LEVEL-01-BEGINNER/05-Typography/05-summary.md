# Summary: Tailwind CSS Typography

Here is a quick cheat sheet of what we covered in this lesson:

* Size: text-xs, text-sm, text-base, text-lg, text-xl... to text-9xl.
* Weight: font-thin, font-light, font-normal, font-bold, font-black.
* Line height: leading-none, leading-tight, leading-normal, leading-loose.
